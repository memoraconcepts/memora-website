Signature service

Experiential, end to end

Questions

Activation questions we hear often

What does a brand activation in Kampala cost?

Memora's event packages start from UGX 800,000. Larger builds are quoted per brief: a design fee, production at cost and a management fee, so you can see where the money goes.

Do you handle production and set-up, or only design?

Both. We design in 3D, manage fabrication and print through vetted Kampala partners, then install and supervise on site.

Can you work under our agency's name?

Yes. Our agency design desk produces key visuals, 3D renders and decks for BTL, event and PR agencies under their name, with a fixed rate card and an NDA as standard.

Are you planning for AFCON 2027?

Yes. We offer an AFCON 2027 kit for fan zones, viewing-party packs and match-day content for June and July 2027. Planning early secures the best sites and partners.
