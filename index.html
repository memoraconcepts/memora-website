What we're watching in Uganda

Short reads for brand teams planning the next twelve months.

10.8Mon TikTok

TikTok is now Uganda's biggest social app

UCC's Q1 2026 data puts TikTok ahead of WhatsApp (9.9M) and YouTube (6.5M). Short vertical video is no longer optional for consumer brands.

Source: UCC via Dignited
19 Jun2027

AFCON 2027 is the activation season to plan for now

Uganda co-hosts with Kenya and Tanzania, with matches in Kampala, Hoima and Lira. Fan zones and viewing parties will need branding months ahead.

Source: AllAfrica
3 GBa month

Make it light

Average data use per subscriber fell from 5.3 GB to 3 GB a month in early 2026. Short clips and WhatsApp Status beat long, heavy video.

Source: UCC via Dignited

Questions

Good to know before we start

What does Memora do?

Memora Concepts Ltd is a branding and experiential agency in Namugongo, Kampala. We design brand identities and logos, brand activations and event branding, and TikTok-first social media content.

Where is Memora based?

Our studio is in Namugongo, Kampala, Uganda. You can reach us on WhatsApp at +256 784 215 671 or by email at info@memoraconcepts.com.

How much does a logo or brand identity cost?

A logo starts from UGX 400,000, a full brand kit from UGX 1.5M and a company profile from UGX 500,000.

How much does a brand activation or event package cost?

Event packages start from UGX 800,000. Full experiential builds are quoted per brief: a design fee, production at cost and a management fee.

Do you offer monthly design and content plans?

Yes. Monthly plans for SMEs, NGOs and schools start from UGX 600,000 a month and can be paused or cancelled anytime. Our first 10 clients get 50% off the first month and a price locked for life.

Can Memora work under our agency's name?

Yes. Our agency design desk creates key visuals, adaptations, 3D renders and decks for BTL, event and PR agencies under their name, with a fixed rate card and an NDA as standard.
