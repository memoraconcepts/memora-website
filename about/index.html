About Memora

Most brands are seen. Few are remembered.

Memora Concepts Ltd is a Ugandan branding and experiential agency based in Namugongo, Kampala. We design brand identities, campaigns and live brand moments that people carry home in their memory.

Who we are

From the logo to the launch night

We work with brand teams, agencies, energy companies, schools and growing businesses. Our team has designed key visuals, activation set-ups and presentations for some of Uganda's best-known consumer brands, much of it alongside partner agencies.

We bring that standard to every client, from a national launch to a neighbourhood business finding its look. Activation, brand and content sit in one accountable team, so the idea on the screen is the same idea people walk into at the event.
