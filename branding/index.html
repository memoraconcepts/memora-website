What we do

A brand people recognise at a glance

Memora designs brand identities for Ugandan companies, from a new business choosing its first logo to an established firm that has outgrown its look.

We start with strategy: who you serve, what makes you different and where your brand will be seen. Then we design a system that works everywhere it needs to, from a WhatsApp profile picture to a billboard, a tender document or a branded vehicle.

  • Brand strategy and positioning
  • Logo design and brand marks
  • Full brand kits: colours, type, patterns
  • Brand guidelines
  • Packaging and labels
  • Company profiles and capability statements
  • Annual reports, brochures and catalogues
  • Signage, stationery and email signatures

Questions

Branding questions we hear often

How much does a logo cost in Uganda with Memora?

A logo starts from UGX 400,000. A full brand kit, with colours, typography, patterns and guidelines, starts from UGX 1.5M. We confirm the price before any work begins.

How long does a logo or brand identity take?

We aim to share first concepts within 48 hours of an agreed brief on plan work. A full brand identity usually takes a few weeks, depending on how many rounds of feedback you need.

Can you design a company profile for tenders?

Yes. We design company profiles and capability statements, including tender-ready profiles for energy, construction and NGO work.

Where is Memora based?

Our studio is in Namugongo, Kampala, Uganda. We work with clients in person and over WhatsApp, email and video calls.
